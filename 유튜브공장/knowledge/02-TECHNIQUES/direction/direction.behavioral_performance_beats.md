# Observable performance beats

## Purpose

Direct emotion through objectives, obstacles, tactics, reactions, and visible behavior rather than labels alone.

## Selection cues

- acting_direction
- reaction

## Directing instructions

- Convert internal emotion into gaze, breath, posture, interrupted action, or a change of tactic.
- Give reactions and listening behavior their own timed beat when they carry meaning.
